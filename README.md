# AI-agent
->>An AI-powered chatbot that analyzes Indian stock market news and explains how global events may impact Indian equities. 


Example Questions

- How will US interest rate cuts affect NIFTY?
- What is the impact of rising oil prices on India?
- How could a recession in the US affect Indian IT stocks?
- What happens to banking stocks when RBI lowers rates?
- How do global geopolitical conflicts impact Indian markets?

Sample Output

Market Sentiment: Bullish

Affected Sectors:
- Banking
- Information Technology
- Energy

Potential Impact:
- Increased foreign investment inflows
- Improved market confidence
- Stronger earnings outlook
